# Legato trademarks

The code is free software under the [AGPL-3.0](LICENSE). The **Legato** name, the Legato logo and wordmark, and the **legato.fm** domain are not. They belong to Daniel Churchill and identify the official project. This page says how you can and can't use them, so people always know whether they're getting the real thing.

## What's covered

- The name "Legato", when it refers to this music software or services built from it.
- The logo and wordmark: `src/assets/brand/`, `src-tauri/icons/`, `public/icons/`, `public/favicon.png` and the marks in `site/public/`.
- legato.fm and its subdomains, including auth.legato.fm.

## You can, without asking

- Say what something is: "a fork of Legato", "based on Legato", "compatible with Legato", "a Legato plugin".
- Use the name and logo to talk about the official project: articles, reviews, tutorials, packages that redistribute **unmodified** official releases.
- Run the official software for yourself, your household or your organisation, under its own name.

## You need permission for

- **Naming a fork or modified build "Legato"**, or anything easily confused with it ("Legato Pro", "OpenLegato", "Legato.something"). A modified build must ship under a different name, with the name, logo and icons replaced. That means updating the files listed above, `tauri.conf.json`'s product name, and the strings the app shows.
- **Offering a hosted service with the name**, such as a relay or a hosted library, or anything that suggests it's run or endorsed by the official project.
- **Using the logo** in a way that suggests sponsorship or endorsement, or in a product's own branding.
- **Registering** a domain, app-store listing or social account that includes "Legato" for a music product.

Ask at [hello@legato.fm](mailto:hello@legato.fm). Reasonable requests are usually fine.

## Why

The AGPL keeps the code open. This policy keeps the name honest. When someone installs "Legato" or connects to a "Legato" relay, they should get the software, and the privacy promises, this project stands behind.
